LTL (Less Than Truckload)

02

Flatbed LTL

LTL flatbed service tailored to your needs, with competitive rates and transit times that keep your shipping cost-effective.

Expedited Services

Same-day, next-day or time-specific delivery. Whether it's time-sensitive to the hour or an emergency, we deliver at the exact time you need.

FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Message our dispatch on WhatsApp and we'll answer right away.

What areas do you serve?

We provide TL and LTL service to and from all points in the GTA and Ontario, with most of our work across the Greater Toronto Area and Southern Ontario.

Do you take partial loads, or only full truckloads?

Both. We run full truckloads and LTL. LTL shipments are palletized before loading and fully secured, from warehouse pickups through to in-store delivery.

What kind of freight do you haul?

We handle all kinds of merchandise, with particular expertise in construction equipment, scaffolding and building supplies.

What if there's no forklift or loading dock at delivery?

We've got it covered. We offer Moffett (truck-mounted forklift) service, straight trucks with tailgates, and pallet jacks or hand assistance to get your cargo off the truck and where it needs to be.

Can you do same-day or urgent deliveries?

Yes. Our expedited service covers same-day, next-day and time-specific delivery, and dispatch is on standby 24/7 for emergencies.

Do you offer storage?

Yes. Through licensed third-party warehouses we store skidded loads and LTL cargo.
